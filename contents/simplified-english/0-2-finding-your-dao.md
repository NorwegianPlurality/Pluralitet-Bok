# Finding Your Dao

Linear books force every reader along the same path. The online version avoids this with hyperlinks, but print readers have fewer options. To help, we structured the text in a "circular" way: you can start at several points, read forward, and loop back to earlier material later.

We suggest:

- If your interest is mainly **current affairs** or politics, start at the beginning with the preface and read straight through.

- If you have a **conceptual or theoretical** interest, consider skipping Parts 1 and 2 and starting at Part 3.

- If you have a **technical** or engineering focus, consider starting at Part 4.

- If you care about specific technologies and their **applications**, consider starting at Part 5.

- If you want to see real-world **impact** in particular social sectors, consider starting at Part 6.

- If your focus is **public policy**, government, or social change, consider starting at Part 7.

No matter where you start, most readers who find value will want to continue reading and loop back to fill in the earlier material.

This book is a living document. If you are reading a printed copy, it is likely already out of date. The latest version is free at https://www.plurality.net/.
