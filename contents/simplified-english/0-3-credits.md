# Credits

The list below shows where ⿻ credits stand in the project today. ⿻ credits are a community currency we used to keep track of who contributed what, and the book explains later how it works. Each contributor appears under exactly one heading, even where they did several different kinds of work. Within each heading, the order of the names and the size of the type show how much ⿻ that person held in early April 2024. The composite image above was built from the same weights. The list leaves out the two main authors, who are covered above in any case, and anyone whose only credit came from endorsing the book, since they are listed in order of credit on the opening pages and on the cover. The technology we used to keep this ledger is experimental, so some entries may be wrong. We are sorry for that, and we will correct them online and in later editions.
